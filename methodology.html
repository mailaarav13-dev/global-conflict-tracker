GLOBAL AFFAIRS TRACKER

Methodology and limitations

This tracker combines an editorial conflict catalog with a frequently refreshed world-news briefing. Conflict profiles and general reporting remain separate so ordinary political, economic, health, technology, climate, and social news is not mislabeled as armed conflict. It is an orientation tool, not an independent verification service or an emergency alert system.

What is updated automatically

News discovery is refreshed by a scheduled background workflow even when nobody has the website open. GDELT supplies multilingual discovery for both conflict-specific reporting and seven world-news areas: politics, security, economy, climate, health, technology, and society. NewsAPI can be enabled as an additional conflict-news provider using a server-side credential. Results are filtered, deduplicated by normalized URL and headline, validated, and saved as a static snapshot.

How Global Pulse works

Global Pulse summarizes the current snapshots into headline counts, publisher diversity, topic activity, recent reporting, and a conflict watchlist. These are transparent coverage summaries rather than an automated judgment about which event is most important. Coverage volume can be influenced by source availability and publisher attention.

What is editorial

Conflict titles, summaries, status labels, historical context, strategic impact, and country associations are maintained in the editorial conflict catalog. They do not change merely because a new headline appears.

Freshness and failures

Country profiles and the world briefing show their snapshot update date. If a provider is unavailable or rate-limits a request, the pipeline retains the last valid articles. The interface labels a snapshot when the last successful update is more than 48 hours old.

Important limitations

  • Inclusion of a headline does not confirm that every claim in the linked article is accurate.
  • Reported casualty figures and territorial claims can change or conflict across sources.
  • Automated keyword matching can omit relevant reporting or occasionally include a marginal result.
  • The publisher-country field describes the news outlet’s location and must not be interpreted as the event location.
  • The absence of a tracked situation does not mean a country is free of political violence or humanitarian risk.

Source transparency

News entries link directly to the original publisher and display the provider and publication date when available. Background citations appear in each conflict profile. Planned structured-data additions include UCDP event data and ReliefWeb, HDX, and UNHCR humanitarian indicators, each with its own coverage period and methodology label.
